# CLAUDE.md — Project Context & Engineering Guidelines for `xero-dorade`

## 1. Project Overview
`xero-dorade` (**XeroDorade**) is an R&D and product engineering repository for a **marine liquid-desiccant dehumidifier and indirect/direct evaporative air conditioner (`LD-IDEC`)** with a **gradual slow-flow Grade 2 Titanium recuperative heat exchanger** and **multi-stage marine intake air pre-filtration**.

Primary reference document: [`liquid-desiccant-idec-hvac-guide.md`](liquid-desiccant-idec-hvac-guide.md).

---

## 2. Core Engineering Rules & Material Constraints (Do Not Violate)

1. **Zero Stainless Steel in Contact with Liquid Desiccant (Titanium & Polymers Only):**
   * Aqueous halide liquid desiccants (`Lithium Chloride LiCl` and `Calcium Chloride CaCl2`) at `55°C–75°C` rapidly destroy `304` and `316L` stainless steel and aluminum via **chloride pitting** and **stress-corrosion cracking (`SCC`)**.
   * Every wetted metallic surface in the liquid desiccant loop (especially the slow-flow sensible heat recuperator and regenerator heater) **must** be **ASTM Grade 2 Commercially Pure Titanium** (or `Grade 7/12 Titanium`). All non-metallic wetted parts must be **`PP`, `PP-R`, `PVDF`, `PTFE`, `PEEK`, `HDPE`, or `EPDM/Santoprene`**.

2. **Heel-Proof Zero-Carryover Hydrophobic Membrane Contactors (No Open Spray Towers):**
   * Never design open packed-bed or falling-film spray contactors that expose free liquid desiccant directly to cabin air on a rolling/heeling boat (`0°–45°` heel).
   * Always specify **Internally Cooled Microporous Hydrophobic Membrane Contactors** (`ePTFE` or `PP` hollow-fiber or flat-sheet membranes, `0.1–0.2 µm` pore diameter, `Liquid Entry Pressure > 2.0 bar`) so water vapor diffuses freely while liquid salt brine is 100% contained without aerosol carryover.

3. **Isothermal Internal Absorber Cooling:**
   * Moisture absorption releases `~2,600–2,800 kJ/kg` of latent + dilution heat. An un-cooled adiabatic absorber quickly heats up, raising the desiccant's equilibrium vapor pressure and stalling dehumidification.
   * The absorber contactor must always be **3-fluid / internally cooled** using `16°C–22°C` water generated by the **Indirect Evaporative Cooler (`IDEC`)** (or pre-cooled by seawater when sea temperatures are `<22°C`).

4. **Gradual Slow-Flow Titanium Counter-Flow Recuperator Design:**
   * Because desiccant mass flow rate is very low (`~10–25 L/hr` or `150–400 mL/min` for a `1.5 kW` cabin unit), the liquid-to-liquid heat exchanger between the **cold dilute stream leaving the absorber (`~24°C`)** and the **hot concentrated stream leaving the regenerator (`~65°C`)** must operate in **strict counter-flow** with a high length-to-diameter ratio (`NTU ≥ 6 to 10`, thermal effectiveness `ε ≥ 88%–92%`).
   * If thermal effectiveness drops below `80%`, hot returning desiccant overwhelms the absorber cooling loop and regeneration heat demand doubles.

5. **Mandatory 3-Stage External Air Pre-Filtration:**
   * All external marine intake air (feeding cabin ventilation `HRV/ERV`, evaporative cooling working channels, and desiccant regeneration scavenge air) must pass through:
     1. **Hydrophobic Salt-Spray Demister / Coalescer** (drains liquid spray),
     2. **Pleated Hydrophobic `ePTFE` `MERV 14` (`ISO ePM1 80%`) Particulate Filter** (blocks dry `NaCl` sea-salt aerosols that would otherwise precipitate in the `LiCl`/`CaCl2` desiccant loop or scale the `IDEC` evaporative wicks), and
     3. **Activated Carbon VOC Stage** (blocks marina diesel soot/odors).
